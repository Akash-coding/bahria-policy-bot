BAHRIA UNIVERSITY — STUDENT LEAVE POLICY
Version 1.0 | Student Affairs

1. Types of Leave
1.1 Short leave: absence of up to 7 consecutive calendar days during a semester.
1.2 Medical leave: absence supported by a medical certificate.
1.3 Emergency leave: sudden family or personal emergency, subject to verification.

2. How Many Leaves a Student Can Take
2.1 A student may take a maximum of 10 working days of short leave in a regular semester, excluding official university holidays.
2.2 Short leave does not waive the 75% attendance requirement. Leave approved under this policy still counts toward attendance calculations unless the absence is classified as official university duty.
2.3 Medical leave beyond 10 working days requires recommendation of the Head of Department and approval of the Director Campus.

3. Application Procedure
3.1 Leave applications must be submitted through the student portal or in writing to the class advisor at least 2 working days in advance, except in emergencies.
3.2 Medical certificates must be submitted within 7 working days of returning to class.
3.3 Leave exceeding 15 consecutive days may require a semester freeze instead of ordinary leave. See the Semester Freeze Policy.

4. Unauthorized Absence
4.1 Absence without an approved leave application may result in disciplinary action under Student Affairs regulations.
4.2 Four consecutive unauthorized absences in a course shall be reported to the Head of Department.

5. Contact
Directorate of Student Affairs, Bahria University.
