BAHRIA UNIVERSITY — EXAMINATION POLICY
Version 1.0 | Examination Policies

1. Examination Structure
1.1 Each credit course is assessed through sessional work (quizzes, assignments, projects), a mid-term examination, and a final examination.
1.2 Unless a course outline states otherwise, the typical weightage is:
    - Sessional work: 30%
    - Mid-term examination: 20%
    - Final examination: 50%
1.3 The passing mark in a course is 50% overall. Failure to appear in the final examination without an approved reason results in an "F" grade.

2. Eligibility
2.1 A student must be registered in the course, have paid the applicable dues, and meet the attendance requirement in the Attendance Policy.
2.2 Students on disciplinary suspension are not permitted to sit examinations during the suspension period.

3. Conduct of Examinations
3.1 Students must bring their university identity card and admit slip.
3.2 Mobile phones, smart watches, and unauthorized notes are prohibited in the examination hall.
3.3 Unfair means (cheating, impersonation, or possession of unauthorized material) shall result in paper cancellation and may lead to rustication as per the Unfair Means Committee decision.

4. Mid-term and Final Schedules
4.1 Mid-term examinations are normally held in week 8 or 9 of a regular semester.
4.2 Final examinations are held after the 16th teaching week according to the date sheet issued by the Examination Department.
4.3 Clash of papers must be reported to the Examination Department within 48 hours of the date sheet being published.

5. Make-up and Rechecking
5.1 A make-up mid-term may be allowed for hospitalization or the death of an immediate family member, with documents submitted within 5 working days.
5.2 There is no make-up for a missed final examination except a genuine medical emergency approved by the Director Campus; in that case an "I" (Incomplete) may be awarded and must be converted within 4 weeks.
5.3 Rechecking of a final paper is limited to totaling errors and unmarked questions. Applications must be filed within 10 working days of result declaration with the notified rechecking fee.

6. Result Declaration
Results are published on the student portal. Grade changes after declaration require approval of the Board of Examiners.
