BAHRIA UNIVERSITY — SEMESTER FREEZE POLICY
Version 1.0 | Academic Policies

1. Purpose
A semester freeze allows a student to pause studies for one regular semester without being treated as withdrawn from the university.

2. Eligibility and Requirements
2.1 A student may freeze a maximum of two semesters during an undergraduate degree, and one semester during a graduate degree, unless the Academic Council grants a special exception.
2.2 The student must have completed at least one regular semester with a CGPA of 2.00 (undergraduate) or 2.50 (graduate).
2.3 First-semester students may freeze only for documented medical or force-majeure reasons approved by the Director Campus.
2.4 Freeze applications must be submitted no later than 15 calendar days after the start of classes. Applications after this date are treated as withdrawal and follow the Fee Refund Policy.

3. Procedure
3.1 The student submits the Semester Freeze Form to the Academic Office with:
    a) written justification
    b) parent/guardian consent for students under 21
    c) clearance from Accounts, Library, and Student Affairs
3.2 The Head of Department recommends the case. The Director Campus grants final approval.
3.3 A freeze fee, as notified in the fee structure for that academic year, is payable if the freeze is approved after fee submission.

4. Return from Freeze
4.1 The student must register in the immediate next regular semester. Failure to register without an approved extension is treated as withdrawal.
4.2 Degree duration limits continue to apply. Frozen semesters count toward the maximum allowed duration unless waived in writing.

5. Transcript Notation
The transcript shall show "Semester Frozen" for the relevant term. No courses may be attended or examined during a frozen semester.
