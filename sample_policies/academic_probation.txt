BAHRIA UNIVERSITY — ACADEMIC PROBATION POLICY
Version 1.0 | Academic Policies

1. Purpose
This policy describes when a student is placed on academic probation and the procedure for returning to good academic standing.

2. Good Academic Standing
2.1 Undergraduate students must maintain a minimum cumulative GPA (CGPA) of 2.00 on a 4.00 scale.
2.2 Graduate students must maintain a minimum CGPA of 2.50 on a 4.00 scale.

3. Placement on Academic Probation
3.1 A student whose CGPA falls below the minimum stated in Section 2 at the end of a regular semester is placed on academic probation.
3.2 The Examination Department issues a written probation notice to the student and copies the Head of Department and the parents/guardian on record.

4. Procedure During Probation
4.1 Students on probation may register for a maximum of 12 credit hours in the following semester (undergraduate) or 9 credit hours (graduate), unless the Head of Department approves otherwise.
4.2 The student must meet the assigned academic advisor at least twice during the probation semester.
4.3 The student must achieve the minimum CGPA in Section 2 by the end of the probation semester, or show semester GPA of at least 2.50 (undergraduate) / 3.00 (graduate) with an improving CGPA.

5. Dismissal After Unsuccessful Probation
5.1 A student who remains below the minimum CGPA after two consecutive probation semesters shall be academically dismissed.
5.2 A dismissed student may apply for re-admission after one regular semester, subject to the Admissions Policy and availability of seats.
5.3 Re-admitted students enter as new students for CGPA calculation unless the Academic Council grants credit transfer.

6. Appeal
6.1 Appeals against probation or dismissal must be submitted to the Director Campus within 10 working days of the notice.
