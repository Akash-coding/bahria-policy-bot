BAHRIA UNIVERSITY — FEE REFUND POLICY
Version 1.0 | Finance Policies

1. Scope
This policy applies to tuition fee refunds for enrolled students who withdraw, freeze a semester, or are found ineligible after fee payment.

2. Refund Schedule for Voluntary Withdrawal
Refunds of tuition fee are calculated from the official start date of classes:
2.1 Withdrawal before the start of classes: 100% tuition fee refund, minus a processing charge of PKR 5,000.
2.2 Withdrawal within the first 7 calendar days of classes: 75% tuition fee refund.
2.3 Withdrawal from day 8 to day 15: 50% tuition fee refund.
2.4 Withdrawal after day 15: no tuition fee refund.

3. Non-Refundable Charges
3.1 Admission fee, registration fee, and security deposit (if already adjusted) are not refundable except where the university is unable to offer the admitted program.
3.2 Hostel and transport fees follow the separate Hostel/Transport refund schedule issued by Administration.

4. Semester Freeze
4.1 If a semester freeze is approved before the start of classes, tuition fee may be transferred to the next registered semester.
4.2 If a freeze is approved after classes have started, the refund schedule in Section 2 applies to the unused portion. See the Semester Freeze Policy.

5. Procedure
5.1 The student submits a refund request on the prescribed form to the Accounts Office with clearance from the library, laboratory, and student affairs.
5.2 Approved refunds are processed within 30 working days by bank transfer to the student's or parent's nominated account.
5.3 Incomplete clearance documents delay the refund until deficiencies are removed.

6. Contact
Accounts Office / Directorate of Finance of the concerned campus.
