BAHRIA UNIVERSITY — ATTENDANCE POLICY
Version 1.0 | Academic Policies

1. Purpose
This policy sets the minimum class attendance requirements for undergraduate and graduate students.

2. Minimum Attendance
2.1 Students must maintain at least 75% attendance in each registered course to be eligible to sit for the final examination.
2.2 Students whose attendance falls below 75% in a course shall be awarded a grade of "W" (Withdrawn) or be barred from the final examination, as notified by the concerned campus.
2.3 Laboratories, studios, and clinical courses may require a higher attendance threshold of 80% where specified in the course outline.

3. Recording of Attendance
3.1 Attendance is marked by the course instructor in each scheduled lecture, lab, or tutorial.
3.2 Late arrival of more than 15 minutes may be recorded as absent at the instructor's discretion.
3.3 Students are responsible for checking their attendance record on the student portal during the semester.

4. Medical and Official Absences
4.1 Absences supported by a medical certificate from a registered medical practitioner, submitted within 7 working days, may be considered by the Head of Department.
4.2 Official university representation (sports, competitions, or assigned duties) may be treated as present if approved in writing by the Directorate of Student Affairs before the event.

5. Warning and Shortfall
5.1 A first warning is issued when attendance falls below 80%.
5.2 A final warning is issued when attendance falls below 75%.
5.3 No re-sit or special examination is granted solely because a student was barred for short attendance.

6. Contact
Questions about this policy may be directed to the Academic Office of the concerned campus.
